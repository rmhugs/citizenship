Nicknames are optional and are never saved. Use first names or nicknames, not anything personal.

How to play

  1. Whoever holds the phone holds the bomb. It has a secret fuse.
  2. Answer the question. Right: pass the phone to the person named on screen. They tap to take it and get a new question.
  3. Wrong: you are locked out for 2 seconds, then you get another question. You keep the bomb.
  4. The fuse keeps burning all the time, even while you pass the phone.
  5. If it blows up while the phone is being passed, the person it is being passed to loses the life, so grab it fast.
  6. Whoever is holding it when it blows loses a life. At 0 lives you are out. Last player standing wins, or the most lives when the bomb limit is reached.
